A3 · Place controls at the correct layer

Open data/bedrock/guardrail-config.json. Guardrail policy, IAM, retrieval trust, deterministic tool authorization, and output validation solve different problems.

Probabilistic controls

Prompt-attack, denied-topic, and sensitive-information assessment can false-positive or false-negative.

Deterministic controls

Resource-scoped IAM, retrieval provenance, tool allowlists, parameter validation, and approval gates decide whether impact is possible.

Input/output hygiene

Strip Unicode tag characters and orphaned surrogates until stable; validate and contextually encode output.

  1. 1 When: ISO time; time also carries epoch milliseconds.
  2. 2 What: class 3002 is Authentication; activity 1 is Logon.
  3. 3 Outcome: the authentication succeeded.
  4. 4 Where: Azure is the provider.
  5. 5 Initiator: the external CI/OIDC workload subject.
  6. 6 Target: the Azure principal obtained by federation.
  7. 7 Source: useful context, but not identity by itself.
  8. 8 Session: connects authentication to later API activity.
  9. 9 Preserved claims: issuer, audience, and role remain available without forcing an uncertain universal mapping.
